Submit

Submit your stories, essays, photographs, comics, doodles, paintings, poems, first-person videos, animations, puzzles, minigames! This is an online archive, designed to be interactive. If you have an idea, reach out.

And if you have a story to tell, but you’re not a writer or an artist, (first ask yourself why you don’t consider yourself one!, and then) consider submitting a straightforward anecdote. Not everything needs to be literarily untouchable. If you’ve told it to your friends, you can tell it (anonymously if you like!) here.

Works will be evaluated for relevance to the following topics:

  • Experiences & emotions surrounding necessary medical procedures
  • Medical care that is underrepresented in any form
  • Experiences in doctors’ offices or hospitals, especially as BIPOC or as a gender minority

Note that we are not looking for stories related to purely cosmetic procedures, except those which you underwent as a child before you were able to consent.

If you’re willing to be contacted by others who might be interested in your experience, feel free to put your contact info, instagram handle, whatever, at the bottom of your piece. If you’d rather be anonymous, that’s fine too.

Send it all to thebodymodarchive@gmail.com! And if you’re interested in collaborating beyond submitting a piece, if you have an idea for improving the site or getting the word out or anything else, please mention this in your email as well.
